Asset Status User Guide

Advantage One · MyGeotab add-in

    Overview

    Asset Status is a MyGeotab page that shows every asset in your database on one screen, with its driver, last report, speed and address, and warns you when a device, camera or driver needs attention.

    Use it to answer three questions quickly:

    • Which assets have stopped reporting, or are reporting bad data?
    • Which assets have a device problem (no GPS, no engine data, failure, removal)?
    • Which drivers have a duty status that does not match what the vehicle is doing, or an HOS violation?

    Everything on the page reads live from your own MyGeotab database with your own sign-in, so you only see the assets and drivers your account is allowed to see. The page never changes any data; it only reads it and links to MyGeotab reports.

    The page at a glance

    The page has four parts, from top to bottom:

    1. Header and title. The Advantage One and Geotab logos sit in the header. Under the Geotab logo, at the right of the page title, are the User Guide and Refresh icons.
    2. Summary boxes. A row of small boxes with counts for your assets, issues and alerts.
    3. Toolbar. Search, group, tracking and last-update filters, the refresh interval, and the Columns button.
    4. Asset table. One row per asset. You choose which columns to show.

    Summary boxes

    The boxes at the top count every active asset in your database. They do not change when you search or filter the table. Archived assets and assets on a Terminated or Suspended plan are left out.

    Box What it shows When it appears
    Assets / Tracked / Untracked Total assets, assets with a telematics device (Tracked), and trailers or equipment without one (Untracked) Always
    Minimums One line per minimum you set, such as "Fuel Level below 20%", with the number of assets under it Only after you set at least one minimum (see Choosing and arranging columns)
    Total Potential Issues The total number of issues, then a count for each of the four issue types Always
    HOS Alerts / Violations Assets whose Duty Status is flagged amber, and assets whose driver has a current violation Only while the Duty Status or Violation column is ticked

    Counts show a dash (—) while data is still loading, or when it could not be read. All boxes keep the same height, and a box is as wide as its longest line.

    An asset with two issues is counted once in each issue's line but twice in the Total Potential Issues number, so the total can be larger than the number of assets affected.

    Finding assets

    The table opens showing only assets that reported today. If an asset you expect is missing, change Last update first.

    Control What it does
    Search Matches part of an asset name, driver name or address. Results narrow as you type.
    Group Limits the page to one MyGeotab group. "All groups" shows everything you can see.
    Show assets All assets, Tracked only (has a telematics device), or Untracked only (trailers, equipment).
    Last update Today, Yesterday, 3 to 5 days, 5 days or more, Never reported, or All. Use "5 days or more" and "Never reported" to find assets that have gone quiet.

    The filters combine: for example, Group "Vancouver" plus Tracked plus "5 days or more" lists only tracked Vancouver assets that have not reported for five days or more.

    Click a column header to sort by it; click again to reverse. Assets with no value in that column always sort last. The table shows 50 assets per page; use the page controls under it to move on. The asset name column is always first and stays in place when you scroll sideways.

    Your Last update choice is remembered in this browser. The other filters reset when you reload the page.

    Choosing and arranging columns

    Click Columns at the right of the toolbar to open the Columns panel. Tick a column to add it to the table and untick it to remove it. Each tick is saved straight away, and the choice applies to everyone who uses this database, not only to you. Press Escape or the x button to close the panel.

    The table starts with Driver, Last Update, Speed and Address. A newly ticked column is added at the end. To move a column, drag its header to a new place. To change its width, drag the edge of its header.

    Group Columns
    Asset Information Driver, Last Update, Speed, Address, Serial Number, VIN, License Plate, Make And Model, Work Requests
    Device Health Potential Issues
    Engine Data Odometer, Engine Hours, Fuel Level, Driver Seatbelt, Coolant Temperature, Battery Voltage, State Of Charge
    Aux Inputs Aux 1 to Aux 4
    Dashcam Camera Health, Live Stream
    ELD Duty Status, Violation
    Winter Operations Solid, Liquid and Prewet Material Rate, Air Temperature, Road Temperature, Third-Party Aux inputs

    Set a minimum

    Fuel Level, Battery Voltage and State Of Charge have a Set min button in the panel. Enter a number (for example 20 for 20 %) and save. From then on, any asset whose reading is below it has that cell tinted light yellow, and a line for it appears in the Minimums summary box. Clear the number to remove the minimum.

    Name an Aux input

    Click the pencil beside an Aux column to give the input your own name (for example "Front Door") and your own words for ON and OFF (for example "Open" and "Closed").

    Potential Issues

    Tick Potential Issues (group Device Health) to see which assets may have a device problem. A flagged cell is tinted light red and shows the issue names with a Click here for details link. The page works the issues out from data it already reads, so treat them as estimates to check, not as confirmed failures.

    Issue Flagged when Suggested action
    No GPS / Location The device is communicating but has no GPS fix, or its position has not changed in 24 hours while the vehicle travelled Remount the device in a clearer location with an unobstructed view of the sky
    No Engine Data The device is communicating and the asset travelled today or yesterday, but none of raw odometer, engine hours, road speed, fuel level or state of charge was reported in the last 7 days Unplug the device for 5 seconds to reset
    Device Failure The device reported a hardware fault in the last 7 days Report to Advantage One Support
    Device Removal The device lost all power three or more times in the last 7 days without a firmware update Inspect the device to ensure it is connected securely

    All issue rules skip assets that last reported 3 days or more ago, since those are better found with the Last update filter. No Engine Data is also skipped for assets on a Base plan.

    The details window

    Click Click here for details to open a window listing each issue on that asset with what was found and the suggested action. Three icons sit beside each issue, from left to right:

    1. Report. Opens the matching MyGeotab report for that asset and the last 7 days in a new tab: Trips History for No GPS, Measurements for No Engine Data, and Engine Faults for Device Failure and Device Removal.
    2. Email. Opens a draft email to Advantage One Support (support@advtracking.net) with the asset, issue and findings filled in.
    3. Copy. Copies the same text so you can paste it into a ticket or message.

    ELD: Duty Status and Violation

    Tick Duty Status and Violation (group ELD) to compare each driver's hours-of-service record with what the vehicle is doing. Both columns follow the driver currently assigned to the asset, so an asset with no driver shows a dash, and untracked assets are blank.

    Duty Status

    The cell shows the driver's latest status: Driving, On Duty, Off Duty, Sleeper Berth, Yard Move or Personal Conveyance. It turns amber and becomes a link when the status does not fit the speed:

    Speed Flagged when the status is
    Over 8 km/h (about 5 mph) Anything other than Driving
    Exactly 0 km/h Driving

    Between 0 and 8 km/h nothing is flagged. Drivers in co-driver (team) mode are never flagged. Click the amber status to open that driver's HOS Logs in MyGeotab for the last 7 days, in a new tab.

    Violation

    The cell shows any HOS violation logged for the driver in the last 24 hours, for example "Driving Limit". It is tinted light red and links to the HOS Violations page filtered to that driver. A dash means no current violation.

    HOS Alerts and Violations counts

    While either column is ticked, a summary box shows HOS Alerts (assets with an amber Duty Status) and Violations (assets with a current violation). A flagged status can be a real problem, or simply a driver who has not changed status yet, so check the HOS logs before acting.

    Other columns and links

    Underlined text in the table is a link. Every link opens in a new tab, so the page stays where you left it.

    Column What it shows Link
    Driver The assigned driver's name. "Assign driver" appears when a tracked asset has none. "Assign driver" opens the asset's page in MyGeotab
    Last Update When the asset last reported None
    Speed Latest speed in km/h None
    Address Street address of the latest position Opens the position in Google Maps
    Work Requests Number of pending repairs A number above zero opens the asset's Maintenance tab
    Camera Health Healthy, Unstable, Unhealthy or Not Working for assets with a paired camera. Unstable, Unhealthy and Not Working are tinted light red. None
    Live Stream "Watch live" for assets with a paired camera Opens the MyGeotab Map with the asset selected

    Untracked assets (trailers and equipment) have no device, so their driver, speed and device columns stay blank. Engine Data, Aux and Winter Operations columns show the latest reading the device reported, or a dash when it has none.

    Refreshing, saved settings and troubleshooting

    Keeping the data current

    The page reloads its data on its own every few minutes. Change Refresh every in the toolbar to any whole number from 1 to 60 minutes, then press Enter. The "Data updated" line under the Refresh icon shows when the table last loaded. To reload the whole page at once, click the Refresh icon at the right of the title; it does the same as F5 in your browser.

    What is saved

    The columns you tick, their order, the minimums, the Aux names and the refresh interval are saved in your MyGeotab database and shared by everyone who uses the page. Only the Last update filter is stored per browser.

    Troubleshooting

    Problem What to check
    An asset is missing Set Last update to All, Show assets to All assets, and Group to All groups. Archived assets and Terminated or Suspended plans are never listed.
    A column shows a dash The device has not reported that reading, or (for Duty Status and Violation) the asset has no driver assigned.
    Counts show a dash Data is still loading or could not be read; wait for the next refresh or click the Refresh icon.
    No Potential Issues for an asset that looks wrong Issues are skipped for assets that last reported 3 or more days ago, and No Engine Data is skipped on Base plans and parked assets.
    Duty Status is amber but the driver is fine The status may not have been updated yet. Open the HOS Logs link to see the driver's actual log.
    A link opens a MyGeotab report with nothing in it The report covers a limited period (24 hours for Violations, 7 days for the others), so older events are not shown.

    For anything else, or to report a Device Failure, email Advantage One Support at support@advtracking.net.
